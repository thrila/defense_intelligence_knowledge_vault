# Elbit Systems

## Overview
- Elbit Systems is an Israeli defense electronics company founded in 1966 and headquartered in Haifa. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Systems)
- The company provides defense electronics, UAVs, and land/air systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Systems)
- It is publicly traded on NASDAQ and the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Systems)
- Elbit develops electronic warfare and ISR systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Systems)
- It serves defense customers worldwide. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Systems)
