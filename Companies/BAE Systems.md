# BAE Systems

## Overview
- BAE Systems is a British multinational defense, security, and aerospace company formed in 1999 and headquartered in London. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems)
- It is one of the largest defense contractors globally. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems)
- The company is publicly listed on the London Stock Exchange (ticker BA.L). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems)
- BAE Systems provides products and services across air, land, sea, and electronic systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems)
- It is involved in programs such as the Eurofighter Typhoon consortium. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems)
