# RTX Corporation

## Overview
- RTX Corporation is a U.S. aerospace and defense company formed in 2020 from the Raytheon and United Technologies merger. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTX_Corporation)
- The company operates through major businesses including Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, and Raytheon. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTX_Corporation)
- RTX is headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTX_Corporation)
- It is publicly traded on the NYSE under ticker RTX. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTX_Corporation)
- RTX produces defense systems and aerospace engines and subsystems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTX_Corporation)
