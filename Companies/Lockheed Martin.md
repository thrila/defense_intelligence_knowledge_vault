# Lockheed Martin

## Overview
- Lockheed Martin is a U.S. aerospace and defense company formed in 1995 and headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin)
- The company operates across aeronautics, missiles and fire control, rotary and mission systems, and space. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin)
- Lockheed Martin is publicly traded on the NYSE under ticker LMT. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin)
- It is a major contractor for military aircraft, missiles, and space systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin)
- The company is the prime contractor for the F-35 Lightning II program. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35_Lightning_II)
