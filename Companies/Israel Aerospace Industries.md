# Israel Aerospace Industries

## Overview
- Israel Aerospace Industries (IAI) is an Israeli aerospace and defense company founded in 1953 and headquartered in Lod. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries)
- The company develops aircraft, drones, missiles, and space systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries)
- IAI is owned by the Israeli government. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries)
- It produces satellites and space systems in addition to defense platforms. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries)
- IAI supplies defense customers in Israel and internationally. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries)
