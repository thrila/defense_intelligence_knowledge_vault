# Northrop Grumman

## Overview
- Northrop Grumman is a U.S. aerospace and defense company formed in 1994 and headquartered in Falls Church, Virginia. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman)
- The company designs and manufactures aircraft, unmanned systems, space systems, and defense electronics. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman)
- Northrop Grumman is publicly traded on the NYSE under ticker NOC. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman)
- Public programs include platforms such as the B-2 Spirit, B-21 Raider, and RQ-4 Global Hawk. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman)
- It serves U.S. and allied defense customers. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman)
