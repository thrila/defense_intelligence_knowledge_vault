# Palantir Technologies

## Overview
- Palantir Technologies is a U.S. software company founded in 2003 and headquartered in Denver, Colorado. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- The company builds data integration and analytics platforms used for government and commercial operations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- Palantir is publicly traded on the NYSE under ticker PLTR. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- Its platforms are positioned around integrating disparate data sources to support decision-making. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- Public disclosures describe products such as Gotham, Foundry, Apollo, and AIP. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)

## Core Products / Platforms
- Gotham: Platform associated with government and defense intelligence workflows. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- Foundry: Platform for enterprise data integration and analytics. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- Apollo: Software delivery platform for deploying Palantir products across environments. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- AIP: Palantir Artificial Intelligence Platform for AI-enabled workflows. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
