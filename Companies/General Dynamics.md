# General Dynamics

## Overview
- General Dynamics is a U.S. aerospace and defense company founded in 1952 and headquartered in Reston, Virginia. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics)
- The company operates across marine systems, combat systems, technologies (C4ISR/IT), and aerospace. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics)
- It is publicly traded on the NYSE under ticker GD. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics)
- General Dynamics is a major builder of submarines and armored vehicles. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics)
- It owns Gulfstream Aerospace as part of its business portfolio. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics)
