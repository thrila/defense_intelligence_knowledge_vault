# Cognite

## Overview
- Cognite is a Norwegian industrial software company founded in 2016 and headquartered in Oslo. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognite)
- The company builds data integration and analytics software for industrial operations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognite)
- Its main product is Cognite Data Fusion, an industrial data platform. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognite)
- Cognite focuses on data contextualization and digitalization for industrial assets. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognite)
- It is privately held. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognite)
