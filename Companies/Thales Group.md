# Thales Group

## Overview
- Thales Group is a French multinational company focused on aerospace, defense, security, and digital identity. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group)
- The company was formed in 2000 and is headquartered in Paris, France. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group)
- Thales provides defense electronics, avionics, and cybersecurity products and services. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group)
- It operates across defense and aerospace markets globally. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group)
- Thales is publicly listed on Euronext Paris. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group)
