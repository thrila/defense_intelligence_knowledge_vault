# Boeing Defense Space and Security

## Overview
- Boeing Defense Space and Security (BDS) is Boeing’s defense, space, and security business unit, established in 2002. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Defense,_Space_%26_Security)
- It is headquartered in Arlington, Virginia and St. Louis, Missouri. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Defense,_Space_%26_Security)
- BDS develops military aircraft, space systems, and defense services. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Defense,_Space_%26_Security)
- As part of Boeing, it is associated with Boeing’s NYSE listing under ticker BA. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Defense,_Space_%26_Security)
- Publicly listed BDS product families include fighters, tankers, patrol aircraft, and space systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Defense,_Space_%26_Security)
