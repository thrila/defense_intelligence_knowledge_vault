# NSO Group

## Overview
- NSO Group is an Israeli technology company founded in 2010 and headquartered in Herzliya. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSO_Group)
- The company is known for developing spyware products such as Pegasus. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSO_Group)
- NSO Group operates in the cybersecurity and intelligence technology space. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSO_Group)
- It is privately held. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSO_Group)
- The company has been the subject of significant public scrutiny and controversy. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSO_Group)
