# Anduril Industries

## Overview
- Anduril Industries is a U.S. defense technology company founded in 2017 and headquartered in Costa Mesa, California. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
- The company builds autonomous systems and software for defense and security applications. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
- Its product portfolio is described publicly as including Lattice, Sentry towers, Ghost drones, and Roadrunner. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
- Anduril focuses on integrating software with hardware platforms for operational missions. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
- It remains privately held. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)

## Core Products / Platforms
- Lattice: Software platform for sensor fusion and autonomous command-and-control. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
- Sentry Towers: Persistent surveillance tower systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
- Ghost: Small unmanned aircraft system. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
- Roadrunner: Autonomous air vehicle system. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
