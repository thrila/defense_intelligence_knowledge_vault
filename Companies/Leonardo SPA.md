# Leonardo SPA

## Overview
- Leonardo SPA is an Italian multinational aerospace, defense, and security company founded in 1948 and headquartered in Rome. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_S.p.A.)
- The company was formerly known as Finmeccanica and rebranded to Leonardo. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_S.p.A.)
- It operates across helicopters, electronics, aircraft, and space systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_S.p.A.)
- Leonardo is publicly listed on the Italian stock exchange (ticker LDO). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_S.p.A.)
- It participates in European defense and aerospace programs. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_S.p.A.)
