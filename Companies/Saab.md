# Saab

## Overview
- Saab AB is a Swedish aerospace and defense company founded in 1937 and headquartered in Stockholm. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_AB)
- Saab produces military aircraft, defense systems, and security solutions. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_AB)
- The company is publicly traded on Nasdaq Stockholm. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_AB)
- Its flagship fighter aircraft program is the JAS 39 Gripen. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_JAS_39_Gripen)
- Saab also builds radar, surveillance, and naval systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_AB)
