# Rafael Advanced Defense Systems

## Overview
- Rafael Advanced Defense Systems is an Israeli defense technology company founded in 1948 and headquartered in Haifa. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Advanced_Defense_Systems)
- The company develops missile defense systems, guided munitions, and defense electronics. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Advanced_Defense_Systems)
- Rafael is known as the developer of Iron Dome. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Dome)
- It operates as a government-owned company. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Advanced_Defense_Systems)
- Rafael serves Israeli and international defense customers. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Advanced_Defense_Systems)
