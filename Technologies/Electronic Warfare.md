# Electronic Warfare

Electronic warfare (EW) is military action involving the use of electromagnetic spectrum to sense, protect, and attack. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_warfare)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Jamming, electronic protection, and electronic support measures. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_warfare)

Key subcomponents:
- Electronic attack, electronic protection, and electronic support. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_warfare)
