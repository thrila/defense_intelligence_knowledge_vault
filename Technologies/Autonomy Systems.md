# Autonomy Systems

Autonomy systems are systems capable of performing tasks with minimal human intervention, often using sensing, planning, and control to operate in real-world environments. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_robot)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Autonomous perception and navigation for unmanned platforms and persistent monitoring. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_robot)

Key subcomponents:
- Perception, localization, planning, and control loops. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_robot)
