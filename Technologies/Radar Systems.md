# Radar Systems

Radar systems use radio waves to detect the range, angle, and velocity of objects. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Air and missile defense, maritime surveillance, and target tracking. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar)

Key subcomponents:
- Transmitter, receiver, antenna, and signal processing chain. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar)

Links to companies using it:
- [[Companies/Lockheed Martin]] builds and integrates radar systems for air and missile defense. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin)
- [[Companies/RTX Corporation]] develops radar and air defense systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTX_Corporation)
