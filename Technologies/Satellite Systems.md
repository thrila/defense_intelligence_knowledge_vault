# Satellite Systems

A satellite is an artificial object intentionally placed into orbit to collect data or provide communications. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Communications, Earth observation, and navigation support. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite)

Key subcomponents:
- Spacecraft bus, payload instruments, and ground segment. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite)
