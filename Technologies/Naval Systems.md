# Naval Systems

Naval systems encompass ships, submarines, and associated combat systems used for maritime operations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_ship)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Surface and undersea warfare, maritime surveillance, and power projection. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_ship)

Key subcomponents:
- Hull, propulsion, sensors, and combat management systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_ship)

Links to companies using it:
- [[Companies/General Dynamics]] builds submarines and naval combat systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics)
- [[Companies/BAE Systems]] builds naval ships and systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems)
- [[Companies/Thales Group]] provides naval sensors and systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group)
