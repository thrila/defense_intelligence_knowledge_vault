# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to systems that perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence such as perception, reasoning, and learning. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Automating perception, decision support, anomaly detection, and mission planning. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)

Key subcomponents:
- Machine learning, knowledge representation, planning, and perception. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)

Links to companies using it:
- [[Companies/Palantir Technologies]] applies AI to operational decision support across data-rich environments. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir_Technologies)
- [[Companies/Anduril Industries]] builds AI-enabled autonomous systems and command-and-control software. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anduril_Industries)
