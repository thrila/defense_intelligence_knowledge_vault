# Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Protecting mission systems, critical infrastructure, and communications networks. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security)

Key subcomponents:
- Network security, identity and access management, and incident response. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security)
