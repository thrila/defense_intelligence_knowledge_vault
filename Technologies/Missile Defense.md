# Missile Defense

Missile defense refers to systems designed to detect, track, intercept, and destroy incoming missiles. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missile_defense)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Layered defense against ballistic, cruise, and tactical missiles. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missile_defense)

Key subcomponents:
- Sensors, command and control, interceptors, and battle management. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missile_defense)

Links to companies using it:
- [[Companies/Lockheed Martin]] provides missile defense systems and interceptors. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin)
- [[Companies/RTX Corporation]] provides air and missile defense systems such as Patriot. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTX_Corporation)
