# Unmanned Aerial Systems

An unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) is an aircraft without a human pilot onboard, typically part of a broader unmanned aerial system (UAS) that includes control links and ground stations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- ISR missions, surveillance, and remote operations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle)

Key subcomponents:
- Air vehicle, sensors/payloads, communications links, and ground control. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle)
