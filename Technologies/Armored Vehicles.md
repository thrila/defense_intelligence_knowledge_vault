# Armored Vehicles

An armored fighting vehicle is a combat vehicle protected by armor and armed with weapons. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armoured_fighting_vehicle)

Typical use in defense/industry:
- Ground combat, troop transport, and battlefield support. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armoured_fighting_vehicle)

Key subcomponents:
- Armor, weapons, mobility systems, and fire control. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armoured_fighting_vehicle)

Links to companies using it:
- [[Companies/General Dynamics]] manufactures armored vehicles such as the M1 Abrams and Stryker. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics)
