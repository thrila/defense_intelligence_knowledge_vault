# Gripen

The Saab JAS 39 Gripen is a Swedish multirole fighter aircraft developed by Saab. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_JAS_39_Gripen)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/Saab]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_JAS_39_Gripen)

Timeline:
- Development began in the 1980s with ongoing production and upgrades. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_JAS_39_Gripen)

Deliverables:
- Multirole fighter aircraft variants used by Sweden and other operators. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saab_JAS_39_Gripen)
