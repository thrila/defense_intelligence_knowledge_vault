# Hermes 450

The Elbit Hermes 450 is a medium-sized unmanned aerial vehicle developed by Elbit Systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Hermes_450)

Parties involved:
- Manufacturer: [[Companies/Elbit Systems]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Hermes_450)

Timeline:
- Introduced in the 1990s with continued use and upgrades. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Hermes_450)

Deliverables:
- UAV platform for ISR missions. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Hermes_450)
