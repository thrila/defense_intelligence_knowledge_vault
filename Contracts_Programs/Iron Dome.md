# Iron Dome

Iron Dome is an Israeli air defense system designed to intercept short-range rockets and artillery shells. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Dome)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/Rafael Advanced Defense Systems]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Dome)

Timeline:
- Development began in the 2000s and the system became operational in 2011. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Dome)

Deliverables:
- Radar, battle management, and Tamir interceptor missiles. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Dome)
