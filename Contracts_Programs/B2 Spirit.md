# B-2 Spirit

The Northrop Grumman B-2 Spirit is a U.S. strategic stealth bomber designed for long-range missions. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-2_Spirit)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/Northrop Grumman]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-2_Spirit)

Timeline:
- Developed during the late Cold War and introduced into service in the 1990s. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-2_Spirit)

Deliverables:
- Stealth bomber aircraft for the U.S. Air Force. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-2_Spirit)
