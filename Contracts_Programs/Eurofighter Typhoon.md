# Eurofighter Typhoon

The Eurofighter Typhoon is a European twin-engine multirole fighter aircraft developed by a consortium of companies and partner nations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurofighter_Typhoon)

Parties involved:
- Industry partners include [[Companies/BAE Systems]], Airbus, and [[Companies/Leonardo SPA]] (through the Eurofighter consortium). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurofighter_Typhoon)

Timeline:
- Development began in the 1980s with ongoing production and upgrades. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurofighter_Typhoon)

Deliverables:
- Multirole fighter aircraft variants operated by multiple European and international customers. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurofighter_Typhoon)
