# Stryker

The Stryker is a family of armored fighting vehicles used by the U.S. Army and other operators. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stryker)

Parties involved:
- Manufacturer: [[Companies/General Dynamics]] (General Dynamics Land Systems). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stryker)

Timeline:
- Introduced in the early 2000s with ongoing upgrades and variants. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stryker)

Deliverables:
- Armored vehicle variants for infantry and combat support roles. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stryker)
