# M1 Abrams

The M1 Abrams is a third-generation American main battle tank designed for modern armored warfare. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_Abrams)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/General Dynamics]] (General Dynamics Land Systems). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_Abrams)

Timeline:
- Entered service in the 1980s with ongoing upgrades. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_Abrams)

Deliverables:
- Main battle tank variants used by the United States and other operators. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_Abrams)
