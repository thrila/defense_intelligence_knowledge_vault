# IAI Heron

The IAI Heron is a medium-altitude long-endurance unmanned aerial vehicle developed by Israel Aerospace Industries. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Heron)

Parties involved:
- Manufacturer: [[Companies/Israel Aerospace Industries]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Heron)

Timeline:
- Developed in the 1990s with multiple variants and export customers. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Heron)

Deliverables:
- UAV platform for ISR and reconnaissance missions. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Heron)
