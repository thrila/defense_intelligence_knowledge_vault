# KC-46 Pegasus

The Boeing KC-46 Pegasus is a U.S. military aerial refueling and transport aircraft derived from the Boeing 767. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_KC-46_Pegasus)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/Boeing Defense Space and Security]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_KC-46_Pegasus)

Timeline:
- The program was awarded in the 2010s with aircraft delivered to the U.S. Air Force. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_KC-46_Pegasus)

Deliverables:
- Aerial refueling and transport aircraft. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_KC-46_Pegasus)
