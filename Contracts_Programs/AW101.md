# AW101

The AW101 is a medium-lift, three-engine helicopter developed by Leonardo (formerly AgustaWestland). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW101)

Parties involved:
- Manufacturer: [[Companies/Leonardo SPA]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW101)

Timeline:
- Development began in the 1980s with operational service starting in the 1990s. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW101)

Deliverables:
- Multi-role helicopter variants used by military and government operators. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW101)
