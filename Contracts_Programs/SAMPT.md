# SAMP/T

SAMP/T is a European medium-range surface-to-air missile system developed by the Eurosam consortium. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAMP/T)

Parties involved:
- Eurosam consortium ([[Companies/Thales Group]] and MBDA). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAMP/T)

Timeline:
- Development began in the 1990s with deployments in the 2000s and later upgrades. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAMP/T)

Deliverables:
- Air defense system using the Aster missile family. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAMP/T)
