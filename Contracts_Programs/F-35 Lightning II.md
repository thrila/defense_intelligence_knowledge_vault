# F-35 Lightning II

The Lockheed Martin F-35 Lightning II is a family of single-seat, single-engine, all-weather stealth multirole combat aircraft developed for the United States and allied partners. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35_Lightning_II)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/Lockheed Martin]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35_Lightning_II)
- Customers: U.S. and partner nations participating in the program. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35_Lightning_II)

Timeline:
- Program development and procurement are ongoing, with multiple variants fielded. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35_Lightning_II)

Deliverables:
- Three main variants (A, B, C) tailored for conventional takeoff, STOVL, and carrier operations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35_Lightning_II)
