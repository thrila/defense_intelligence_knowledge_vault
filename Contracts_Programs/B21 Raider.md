# B-21 Raider

The Northrop Grumman B-21 Raider is a U.S. long-range, stealth strategic bomber under development. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-21_Raider)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/Northrop Grumman]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-21_Raider)

Timeline:
- Program announced in the 2010s with ongoing development and test flights. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-21_Raider)

Deliverables:
- Next-generation stealth bomber for the U.S. Air Force. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_B-21_Raider)
