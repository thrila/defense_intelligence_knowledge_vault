# RQ-4 Global Hawk

The Northrop Grumman RQ-4 Global Hawk is a high-altitude, long-endurance unmanned aerial vehicle used for ISR missions. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_RQ-4_Global_Hawk)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/Northrop Grumman]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_RQ-4_Global_Hawk)

Timeline:
- Development began in the 1990s with ongoing operations and upgrades. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_RQ-4_Global_Hawk)

Deliverables:
- High-altitude ISR unmanned aircraft and support systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_RQ-4_Global_Hawk)
