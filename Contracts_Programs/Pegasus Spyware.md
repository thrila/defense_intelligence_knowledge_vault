# Pegasus Spyware

Pegasus is spyware developed by the NSO Group that has been reported as used to target smartphones. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_(spyware))

Parties involved:
- Developer: [[Companies/NSO Group]]. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_(spyware))

Timeline:
- Reported by the public press and watchdog organizations during the 2010s and 2020s. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_(spyware))

Deliverables:
- Smartphone spyware suite. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_(spyware))
