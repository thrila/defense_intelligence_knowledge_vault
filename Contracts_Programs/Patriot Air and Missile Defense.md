# Patriot Air and Missile Defense

The MIM-104 Patriot is a U.S. surface-to-air missile system and an air and missile defense platform designed to counter aircraft and tactical ballistic missiles. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIM-104_Patriot)

Parties involved:
- Prime contractor: [[Companies/RTX Corporation]] (Raytheon segment). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIM-104_Patriot)

Timeline:
- Development began in the late Cold War period with ongoing upgrades and deployments. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIM-104_Patriot)

Deliverables:
- Radar, command-and-control station, and interceptor missiles as part of the system. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIM-104_Patriot)
