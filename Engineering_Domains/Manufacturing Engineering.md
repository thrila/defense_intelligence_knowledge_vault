# Manufacturing Engineering

Manufacturing engineering is a field of engineering that designs and optimizes production processes and systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing_engineering)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Scaling production of aircraft, missiles, and complex subsystems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing_engineering)

Typical outputs:
- Production lines, tooling, quality systems, and process controls. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing_engineering)

- Wikipedia: Manufacturing engineering. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing_engineering)
