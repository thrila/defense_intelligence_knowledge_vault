# Systems Engineering

Systems engineering is an interdisciplinary approach to enabling the realization of successful systems by focusing on defining customer needs and required functionality early in development. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Integrating complex defense and aerospace systems-of-systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering)

Typical outputs:
- Requirements, architectures, integration plans, and verification/validation artifacts. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering)

Key references:
- Wikipedia: Systems engineering. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering)
