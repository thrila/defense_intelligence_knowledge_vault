# Data Engineering

Data engineering is focused on designing and building systems for collecting, storing, and analyzing data at scale. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_engineering)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Building pipelines and platforms that support analytics, mission systems, and digital twins. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_engineering)

Typical outputs:
- Data pipelines, storage systems, and governance frameworks. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_engineering)
