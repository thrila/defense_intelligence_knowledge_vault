# Aerospace Engineering

Aerospace engineering is the primary field of engineering concerned with the development of aircraft and spacecraft. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineering)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Design and production of military aircraft, missiles, and space systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineering)

Typical outputs:
- Airframes, propulsion integration, flight systems, and mission payloads. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineering)

- Wikipedia: Aerospace engineering. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineering)
