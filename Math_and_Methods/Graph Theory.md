# Graph Theory

Graph theory is the study of graphs, mathematical structures used to model pairwise relations between objects. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_theory)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Modeling networks, supply chains, and relationships in intelligence analysis. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_theory)

Typical algorithms:
- Shortest path, centrality measures, and community detection. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_theory)
