# Control Theory

Control theory studies the behavior of dynamical systems and how to influence them using feedback to achieve desired outputs. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_theory)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Guidance, navigation, and control for aircraft, missiles, and autonomous systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_theory)

Typical algorithms:
- PID control, state-space control, and optimal control. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_theory)

People:
- [[People/Norbert Wiener]]: Cybernetics and feedback foundations. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norbert_Wiener)

Links to companies/products:
- Expected in [[Companies/Lockheed Martin]] and [[Companies/Northrop Grumman]] aerospace systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin)
