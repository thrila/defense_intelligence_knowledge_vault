# Kalman Filtering

The Kalman filter is an algorithm that uses a series of measurements over time to estimate the state of a dynamic system. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalman_filter)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Tracking targets and navigation in aerospace and defense systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalman_filter)

Typical algorithms:
- Standard Kalman filter, extended Kalman filter, and unscented Kalman filter. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalman_filter)

People:
- [[People/Rudolf Kalman]]: Namesake of the Kalman filter. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_E._K%C3%A1lm%C3%A1n)

Links to companies/products:
- Expected in [[Companies/Lockheed Martin]] and [[Companies/RTX Corporation]] tracking systems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalman_filter)
