# Optimization

Optimization (mathematical optimization) is the process of finding the best solution from a set of feasible options, often by minimizing or maximizing an objective function subject to constraints. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_optimization)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Scheduling, resource allocation, and mission planning problems. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_optimization)

Typical algorithms:
- Linear programming, nonlinear optimization, and integer programming. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_optimization)
