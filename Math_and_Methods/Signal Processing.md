# Signal Processing

Signal processing is the analysis, interpretation, and manipulation of signals such as sound, images, and sensor data. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_processing)

Where it shows up in these industries:
- Radar, communications, and ISR sensor pipelines. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_processing)

Typical algorithms:
- Filtering, Fourier transforms, and spectral analysis. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_processing)

People:
- [[People/Claude Shannon]]: Information theory underpinning modern signal processing. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Shannon)

Links to companies/products:
